## Design Context — "Reel"

This file is the site's design context. "Reel" was developed on the `creative` branch and merged into `main` on 4 October 2026, replacing the strict monochrome technical monograph (STIX Two Text + B612 Mono) that is in the history before the merge; none of the monograph's rules apply. The "Never break" rules in `.design/CREATIVE_BRANCH_BRIEF.md` still apply: facts only from `.design/DOSSIER.md` and the source, no phone numbers, the placeholder gate, the checker at 0 errors, WCAG 2.2 AA, and a working TMDS demo and 3D board.

### Users
Primary: internship recruiters and hiring managers in hardware / embedded / FPGA work. They arrive from a LinkedIn or CV link, on a laptop, and give the site about sixty seconds before deciding whether to read a case study. Secondary: TU/e staff and professors, who will read a case study end to end and check the claims against what they know; fellow students and Student Team Daedalus members, who want to see the builds. The job to be done: decide quickly whether this second-year EE student can be trusted with real hardware, then find the evidence.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **verified, hands-on, unhurried**, the same as main. On this branch the site is also allowed to be memorable. It should feel hand-made and specific to him, never a template, and the thing people remember should be his own evidence, not an effect. Voice: first person, plain, specific, no hype vocabulary, American spelling.

### Aesthetic Direction — "Reel"
Chosen 27 Sep 2026 from five directions (A Bit field, B Diptych, C Drawing sheet, D Plates & tape, E Reel) shown side by side with the real content. The owner's words: "I like E the most. Maybe add the bit map for the hdmi". Then, from three variants of E (E1 type scale, E2 color temperature, E3 density), the owner picked **E2, the warm palette**, because it "has more consistency in the color scheme". The type scale and density stay as in the original E: names at about 6% of the window width, and one line of spec per project.

- **Home: about him first, then the work to choose from** (owner, 27 Sep 2026: "I want the main page to be an introduction to me … where they can decide what project to move to"; the first version, a one-project-per-screen reel that opened on the HDMI bit field, was dropped).
  - The intro, on paper: his name set wide, the one-sentence lead, two paragraphs about how he works (the whole length of a device; verification before blinking), and a facts list (study and average, team, current and next role, languages, work authorization, schooling), with links to the CV.
  - The work: one full-width band per project, each on the ground its case study uses, carrying its number, its name set wide, one line, stack · dates · status, and a glimpse of its evidence (the XSIM frame in its envelope, the 3D board, the to-scale drawings). The whole band opens the case study. No project is the headline; they are listed in case-study order (01 HDMI, 02 Venus, 03 HiFi, 04 Rock Your Baby), then the Daedalus team band, then the five smaller builds as a line of links.
- **The HDMI bit field** (from direction A, added on 27 Sep and then removed from the home page the same day at the owner's request). Its code is in `src/scripts/bits.js`, not loaded by any page.
- **Four-corner chrome on every page:** the name top left; Work, Notes, CV from the center (Work was "Index" until 4 Oct 2026, renamed so the link says what it opens); Contact top right, which jumps to the Contact footer (the confirmed internship line appears there, once per page).
- **Build names set wide.** Anybody at weight 800, width 150: extended lettering like the panels of hi-fi and test equipment. Chrome uses Anybody at widths 100–112. Spec lines and readouts are in **Azeret Mono**. Two families, self-hosted in the real build.
- **A warm ground and ink per project (E2).** The palette is 1970s hi-fi: walnut, sand, dusk.
  - Grounds: HDMI warm black `#120E0B`, HiFi paper `#F3EEE4`, Venus sand `#E7DBC3`, Rock Your Baby walnut `#241B15`, Daedalus dusk `#E8D2AE`. Pages that are not one project use paper (work index, CV, log) or dusk (notes), and the 404 uses warm black.
  - Inks: warm white `#F4ECDF` on the dark grounds, brown-black `#1C150F` on the light ones.
  - Quieter inks: `#B3A796` on the dark grounds, `#5E5244` on the light ones, each checked for AA on every ground where it appears.
  - The only saturated color is data, kept pure: the red in the XSIM frame.

References, the owner's own list (notes in `.design/references/`): smorgasbord.studio and specialoffer.inc (four-corner chrome, the evidence framed three ways), this.design (ground and ink per project), thereseoflisieux.co.nz (index entries as a large title over one line), kommakomma.is (spec-sheet case page).
Anti-references (the owner's words): corporate templates, AI-looking gradients, too much symmetry, glassmorphism, dashboard idioms (hero metrics, badges, progress bars that aren't progress), dark mode with glowing accents.

### Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA:
- Every text/ground pair checked on every ground (measured in the header of the generated `tokens.css`, and asserted by the contrast test in `packages/tokens`).
- A visible focus ring in the ground's ink; on the home bands it is drawn inside the band.
- A skip link and alt text on every image (the evidence glimpses on the home bands are decorative: the band's text says what the project is).
- Nothing moves on its own; no information exists only on hover.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, every transition is instant.
- The site works without JavaScript.

### Design Principles
1. **The person, then the evidence.** Home introduces him and lets the reader choose; each case study then leads with its real artifact (the frame, the encoder demo, the board, a to-scale drawing).
2. **Figures from real numbers.** Every drawing comes from the source: timing in clocks, codewords from the encoder, cubes to scale. If a number is not in the source, the figure does not show it, and status is stated plainly ("in progress", "nothing assembled yet").
3. **Nothing moves on its own.** The only motion is a state change the reader asked for.
4. **Chrome follows the ground.** One ground and one ink per project, contrast-checked, with no scrims and no text over busy pixels.
5. **Big or small, nothing in between.** Names are huge and wide; everything else is small and quiet. There is no third size for decoration.
